A tiny Mac menu bar app that bridges compatible SMART+ Wi-Fi bulbs and light strips into Apple Home — including models without native HomeKit support. Set it up once, then control your lights with Home, Siri, scenes, and automations.

## Set up in minutes

1. **[Download the Mac app](https://github.com/ferdinandl007/smartplus-homekit-bridge/releases/latest)** and move it to Applications.
2. **Log in to your SMART+ account.** Your lights are found automatically.
3. **Scan the QR code in Apple Home.** You're connected.

## Set it and forget it

- **Made for Apple Home.** Power, dimming, supported colors, and a dedicated white mode for RGBW lights.
- **New lights? Already handled.** Supported lights are added automatically as they come online.
- **Local control.** Light commands stay on your home network.
- **Quiet by design.** Just connection status and light count in your menu bar.
- **At home on your Mac mini.** Launch at login and keep the bridge available in the background.
- **Free and open source.** No subscription.

**Made for compatible SMART+ Wi-Fi bulbs and light strips.** Not all OSRAM / LEDVANCE products support HomeKit; this app provides a bridge for supported Wi-Fi models, not the entire product range. Zigbee, Bluetooth-only, and other device types are not supported.

For Apple silicon Macs running macOS 15 or later. Keep the host Mac powered and connected.
